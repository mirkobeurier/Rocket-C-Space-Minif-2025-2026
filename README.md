# C-Space-Minif-2025-2026

Design of a 1 m tall rocket for the C'Space rocket challenge

## Overview
Organized by the French Space Agency (CNES) and the Planète Sciences association, C’Space offers young people the opportunity to bring to life the projects they have developed throughout the year. Rocket launches, CanSat drops, and experimental balloon releases punctuate this week-long gathering, which brings together students and professionals from diverse cultural and scientific backgrounds.

## Objectives
- Roll control on ascent
- Landing legs
- Recovery of data
- Light pollution detection

## Features
- 1 meter
- 1.6 kg of max mass
- 2 parachutes for descent
- 3D printed parts

## Tools used
- 3D exp
- Fusion 360
- OpenRocket
- 3D printing

## My role
- Design and module repartition inside the rocket
- Universal module design (Structure, Power...)
- Fin roll stabilisation
- Structural components design(Fins, Motor structure...)
